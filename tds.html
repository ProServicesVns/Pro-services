Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is the main change in TDS under the Income Tax Act 2025?

A: The primary change is the consolidation of various TDS sections (like 194C, 194J, 194H) into a unified structure under Section 393. This structural change aims to simplify the code while maintaining distinct rates for different types of payments.

Q2: How do I find the new section for 194C (Contractors)?

A: Under the new ITA 2025, payments to contractors are mapped to Section 393(1)[6(i)]. Our tool provides the exact sub-clause mapping for both individuals and other entities.

Q3: Are the TDS rates changing for FY 2026-27?

A: Yes, several rates have been streamlined. For instance, Rent and Commission sections have seen adjustments in their mapping and thresholds. Always refer to the "Rate" column in our tool for the latest assessment year data.

This registry is designed for Chartered Accountants, Tax Professionals, and Finance Managers to quickly transition from the 1961 Act to the 2025 Act. To use:
